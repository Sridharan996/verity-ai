## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, institutional, yet technologically progressive identity for statutory technical education regulation. It bridges sovereign authority with next-generation automated verification.

### Personality & Emotional Response
The interface evokes absolute rigor, institutional credibility, transparent governance, and effortless speed. It eliminates bureaucratic friction while retaining legal gravity. Users experience a feeling of clean order, unyielding auditability, and intelligent assistance.

### Design Movement: Modern Precision Glassmorphism & Sovereign Utility
The design language combines structured Swiss typography, refined enterprise data density, and delicate frosted-glass surface architecture (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`). It utilizes light translucent layering, subtle hairline borders (`#E2E8F0`), and sovereign color anchors—combining deep sovereign navy with regal amber and saffron accents.

## Colors

The palette balances sovereign dignity with instant cognitive readability across complex verification workflows.

### Primary & Brand Anchors
- **Primary Navy (`#0A192F`):** Anchors navigation, prime actions, high-emphasis text, and regulatory headers.
- **Executive Navy Surface (`#1E293B`):** Employed for dark sub-menus, table headers, and focused states.
- **Sovereign Gold / Saffron (`#D97706`):** Symbolizes institutional sanction, official seals, active steps, and cautionary notices. Secondary highlight gold: `#F59E0B`.

### Functional Semantic Hues
- **Verified Emerald (`#059669` / `#10B981`):** Applied to cleared scrutiny items, passed inspections, and verified documents.
- **Flagged / Deficiency Crimson (`#DC2626` / `#EF4444`):** Flags compliance breaches, expired documents, and critical anomalies.
- **System Blue (`#2563EB` / `#3B82F6`):** Highlights clickable data identifiers, progressive flow nodes, and secondary CTAs.

### Surfaces & Neutral Canvas
- **App Canvas (`#F8FAFC` to `#F1F5F9`):** Cool slate ground.
- **Frosted Glass Containers:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)` with `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.85)`.
- **Text Tiers:** `#0F172A` (900 - Headings), `#334155` (700 - Body Primary), `#64748B` (500 - Metadata & Captions).

## Typography

The pairing combines **Plus Jakarta Sans** for authority-driven titles, institutional headers, and scorecard figures with **Inter** for dense tabular datasets, inspector notes, and form structures.

### Hierarchy & Functional Rules
- **Numerical Indicators:** All tabular numbers, application IDs (`AICTE-EOA-2026-001`), and scorecards use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to preserve spatial alignment in comparisons.
- **Section Headers:** Sub-headers employ semi-bold styling with subtle letter spacing to separate complex institutional validation modules.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column adaptive grid governs regulatory console views, transitioning to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 

### Structural Layout Model
- **Left Navigation Rail:** Fixed at 240px on desktop screens (`≥1280px`), collapses to a floating icon-rail (72px) on medium screens (`1024px–1279px`), and shifts to a slide-over drawer below `1024px`.
- **Dual Pane Workspaces:** The document validation engine splits into a dual 50/50 workspace (Document Viewer on the left, Validation Scrutiny Checklist on the right) with synchronized scrolling.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm:** Modules adhere strictly to an 8pt base grid for component heights, table rows (48px standard, 40px compact), and card containers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses frosted glass layers rather than heavy opaque dropshadows, ensuring maximum legibility across complex multi-panel dashboards.

### Layering Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Ground):** `#F8FAFC` flat canvas with faint sovereign watermark or geometric background patterns.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Modules):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` fill, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, bounded by a crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0` border. Shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Panels & KPI Cards):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`, `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Overlays, Document Inspection Zoom & Popovers):** Solid `#FFFFFF`, `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The interface balances government authority with software accessibility through controlled geometric curvature.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Used for input fields, table action buttons, data chips, and status badges.
- **Large Radius (`1.0rem` / `16px`):** Used for primary glass cards, AI inspection viewports, scorecard containers, and preview modals.
- **Badge & Status Roundedness:** Full pill shape (`9999px`) reserved specifically for verification pill tags (`Verified`, `Flagged`, `Under Review`, `Pending`) and user avatars to distinguish metadata from operational buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Sovereign Action:** `#0A192F` background, white label, subtle top inner-bevel (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.15)`), 8px border-radius, 40px height. Hover: `#1E293B`.
- **Accent / Saffron Action:** `#D97706` background, white label. Used for primary regulatory dispatch, submissions, and document generation.
- **Secondary Ghost / Glass:** White glass surface with `#E2E8F0` border, `#0A192F` text, hovering to `#F1F5F9`.

### Status Badges & Audit Chips
Pill-shaped badges (`rounded-full`) with a leading 6px colored indicator dot:
- **Verified:** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, dot `#10B981`.
- **Flagged / Deficiency:** Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, dot `#EF4444`.
- **Under Review:** Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, dot `#F59E0B`.
- **Pending Action:** Background `#EFF6FF`, text `#1E40AF`, dot `#3B82F6`.

### Scrutiny Tables & Data Lists
- Table headers use uppercase text (`11px`, `font-weight: 600`, color `#64748B`, background `#F8FAFC`).
- Row borders are hairline `#F1F5F9`. Hover transitions row surface to `rgba(241, 245, 249, 0.6)`.
- Numeric references (application numbers, academic years) use tabular Inter mono-styling.

### Document Inspection Viewer
- Embedded document canvas framed with `#0A192F` dark chrome container.
- Verification bounding boxes overlay flagged sections with 1.5px crimson borders and an amber caution tooltip for AI extraction discrepancies.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Form controls feature 38px height, `#FFFFFF` solid fill with `#CBD5E1` outline, shifting to a 2px `#2563EB` glow ring upon focus.
- Micro-labels sit directly above input fields in semi-bold `#334155`.
